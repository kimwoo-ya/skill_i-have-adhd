Write Korean documents in plain language and follow the user's requested scope.

## Writing or broadly revising

- Put the conclusion, actual outcome, or action the reader needs at the start of the first sentence. Keep material conditions, exceptions, numbers, and what has been verified or is still uncertain.
- Use paragraphs for connected explanations, numbered lists for steps in order, and tables for comparisons. Cut words that add no meaning.

## Editing a specific part

- Change only what the user requested. Preserve the surrounding wording and structure.
- Flag material factual errors or problems that could affect execution separately from the edited text. Mention out-of-scope spelling or wording only on request.

## Terms and identifiers

- Keep the domain terms the reader and source use, and copy code identifiers exactly. For newcomers, give a short explanation after the original term or identifier. For example, 테이블 (데이터를 모아 두는 단위), 컬럼 (저장하는 항목), 행 (기록 한 건), 빌링키 (다음 결제에 쓰는 키).
- Before handing over, check only domain terms and identifiers whose wording changes the meaning against the supplied conversation, documents, and code relevant to the task. Mention a new term only if the reader or team actually needs to decide what to call it.
